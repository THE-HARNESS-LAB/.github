### [TitleDesk Agent](https://github.com/THE-HARNESS-LAB/titledesk)

The title workstation for petroleum landmen. Reads county records on your own
machine, computes ownership with exact fractions, and produces the deliverable
with every number traceable to its source.

Roughly **60–75% of the hours in a title project are mechanical** — indexing
records, cross-referencing instruments, recomputing fractions, retyping it all
into somebody's workbook. None of it requires judgment. TitleDesk does that part.

## How we build

**Local first.** The records stay on your computer. Title files contain client
business, mineral ownership, and personal information that has no business being
uploaded to somebody's cloud in order to be read.

**Traceable.** Every figure in a deliverable points back to the page it came
from. If a number can't be traced, it doesn't belong in the report.

**Exact.** Ownership is computed in exact fractions, not floating point. A
decimal that drifts in the sixth place is a decimal that gets argued about in
a title opinion.

**Deliverable-shaped.** The output is the thing that actually gets turned in —
in the format the client asked for — not an export you have to rebuild by hand.
